# Context Profile Assessment
The chapter's stance toward people who leave the industry — "I understood. I did." followed by "This job asks for everything... the only thing it gives back is the work itself" — is exactly the voice profile's required blend of respect and unsentimentality (§3, §11.1: "Not everyone stays. Some burn out. Some move on. This is part of the realism."). No superhero narrator; the chapter explicitly frames the narrator's endurance as personal disposition ("For me, it was becoming everything"), not virtue.

# Voice Standard Assessment
The chapter is a compressed-time bridging piece, which by its nature summarises more than it scenes — that's a legitimate structural choice, not automatically a defect (the entry-type guide lists this pattern). But several sentences drift toward generic memoir-summary cadence rather than the sharper, image-anchored prose of 07 and 10 — e.g., "None of it came from a textbook. It came from repetition. From fucking up and fixing it." This is serviceable but noticeably plainer than the manuscript's strongest passages.

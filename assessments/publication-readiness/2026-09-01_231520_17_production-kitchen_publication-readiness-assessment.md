# Context Profile Assessment
This chapter is one of the batch's strongest for narrator voice. The shift from confident CDP to bewildered new arrival is rendered entirely through specific, concrete disorientation — "I don't think I've ever sat down in uniform before," the frozen fish in a sink ("like it was some kind of fucking baptism"), the vege-stick precision mismatch with Dane and Robbie ("Near enough" vs. one-centimetre batons). Present tense is used deliberately for punch in the internal-monologue asides ("*What the fuck is this.*" / "*This may possibly be the worst decision I've ever made.*"), consistent with the profile's "present tense used for punch, emphasis, or universal truths" (§1). No sanitising, no sitcom-chaos — the "near enough" sous chefs are drawn as a plausible cultural mismatch, not incompetent clowns.

# Voice Standard Assessment
No introduced defects found. The chapter never states what the narrator feels about the standards gap — it renders it entirely through the fish-in-the-sink discovery, the "vege sticks" language mismatch, and Dane holding up a baton "like he'd never seen one before." This is the profile's "Emotional Core (Show, Don't Declare)" standard executed cleanly, with none of the 11–13/14 pattern's "I realised" or thesis-explaining constructions present.

# Voice Standard Assessment
Two clear rendering-vs-stating instances:
1. "internally acknowledging that I am absolutely not built for the level of responsibility that pot required" — states the self-doubt directly rather than showing it through action or detail.
2. "Feeling almost... capable?" — again, names the feeling (with a hedge/question mark that softens but does not remove the violation).

`CLAUDE.md`'s Hard "No" List states explicitly: "Stating emotions instead of rendering them ('I felt proud')." These lines are the same construction with different wording ("I am... not built for," "Feeling almost... capable") and belong to the same category of defect. This is the single strongest finding in this chapter and the reason its score is capped at Significant Revision rather than Minor.

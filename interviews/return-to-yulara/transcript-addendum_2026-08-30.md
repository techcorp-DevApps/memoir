## Segment 12 — Stacey at the pass, before and after the opening

Given by Bosco, 2026-08-30, in answer to **B8** (whether Jackson's birth stays at
three sentences). His ruling was **expand**, and this is the material behind it.

**As given — preserve the beats, not necessarily the phrasing:**

> when stace was heavily pregnant she would come and sit in the kitchen on a milk
> crate and have dinner after work (before dinner service kicked off) as it was
> the only way she was able to see me in those few prior to opening and the weeks
> immediately after opening, she was treated like royalty kitchen stewards would
> see her coming and run to me announcing her arrival, my CDP Navi would begin
> making her dinner, stace was always very proud of my status in the kitchen and
> enjoyed being able to watch me in those moments

**The beats, unpacked:**

- **Stacey, heavily pregnant**, came into the kitchen and sat on **a milk crate**.
- She ate **after her own work**, and **before dinner service kicked off** — the
  window between prep finishing and the first tickets.
- It was **the only way she got to see him** in the weeks before the opening and
  the weeks immediately after.
- **She was treated like royalty.** The **kitchen stewards** would spot her coming
  and **run to him to announce her arrival**.
- **Navi** — his CDP — would start making her dinner without being asked.
- Stacey was **proud of his status in that kitchen**, and liked being able to
  **watch him work**.

**Why it earns its place, in his words:** *"an important milestone and there is
actually valuable weight the context contains which will only increase the ability
to visualise the scene when reading."*

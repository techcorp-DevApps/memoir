## Key Decisions Made

1. **Explicit criminal history**: Full detail in origin arc allows later oblique references (FAT SAM etc.) to carry weight without heavy exposition

2. **Age correction**: 19 at sentencing, not 23

3. **Title pattern**: "CATCH YA ON THE FLIPSIDE" chosen over "THE GOOD ONE" — human anchor (brother) over legal technicality (concurrent), follows existing title pattern

4. **Home detention teaser**: Changed from full exposition to "It didn't come. But we'll get to that." — plants seed without giving away

5. **Ending line**: Changed from "I'd done worse for less" to "I'd been through worse. ... Or had I?" — uncertainty rather than false bravado

6. **Voice standard**: Each piece must generate "emotional rush, goosebumps, weight" — historically factual without being boring

## Quality Standard

Each piece must:
- Generate the emotional rush, goosebumps, weight experienced in finalized chapters
- Be historically factual without being boring
- Maintain voice profile adherence (chef_writer_context_profile.md v2.1)

# Chapter Numbering — 9 September 2026

## Governing decision

`STEPS TO FREEDOM` occupies chapter 05, immediately after `FOUR FLIGHTS` and
immediately before `STARTING MONDAY`.

`STARTING MONDAY` moves from 05 to 06. Its opening release, original-parole-officer
and policy material now closes `STEPS TO FREEDOM`, so chapter 06 begins:

> The first time I met her, I was ten minutes late.

draft material exists, but that chapter has not yet been reconstructed through the
current interview process. No stub prose has been invented to fill it.

## Historical-file rule

Dated assessments, remediation reports, archived recovery files and the recovered
v1.2.0 drafts retain the numbers they carried when created. Those filenames are
historical evidence, not the current contents list. Their internal references must
be read against this numbering record.

Only current chapter filenames and current governing documents have been aligned to
the new numbering.

chapter is written toward it.

block sits in front of the courtroom chapter — see PART ZERO. So the rule is not
"no fixed ending"; it is **no fixed extent**. Scope has grown at both ends and the
plan should stop being surprised by it.

## PART SEVEN: AFTER THE LADDER

**Ruled 30 August 2026 (ledger B30).** The Jackson material sits **after** §23, not
inside it.

§23 names ChefTip #1 and refuses the story behind it — *"I'm not going to tell you
some long story here."* That is the design. The reader leaves the career arc
wondering what cost that tip so much, and gets the answer here, retrospectively.

Bosco's reasoning, and it governs how this part is written:

> *"It will allow for the career period and kitchen content to phase out naturally…
> It also removes some pretty heavy content from the career [arc], and while in real
> life it did suffocate it and ultimately result in not returning to the industry, I
> don't want it to take away from the reader."*

**Three rules follow, and they bind every chapter before this part:**

1. **No career chapter anticipates the death.** No shadow, no foreshadowing, no
   dramatic irony. The Yulara boundary — Jackson is born there and does not die
   there — now extends across the whole manuscript. The reader is not being set up;
   they are being left to wonder.
2. **The heavy content stays out of the career arc entirely.** It suffocated the
   career in life. On the page it is not permitted to suffocate the reading.
3. **§23 is unblocked.** It was gated on the 11 DECIDE items in
   `interviews/jackson/CORRECTION-LEDGER.md` because it was to host the verbatim
   material. It no longer does. Those items gate **this part only.**

### The ending, in his words — and it is the thesis of the book

> *"Once again returning to the workforce in a new line of work. No chasing a
> career — just turn up, do the job and go home. Not what it used to be, and the
> rush of a service is missed. The brutality in love of a kitchen. Felt like part of
> the soul has gone. But it allows for the time to be spent with those that matter,
> just as ChefTip #1 advises."*

**That is the book closing its own loop.** ChefTip #1 is stated at §23 as a lesson.
It is *lived* here, at cost, in a job he does not love. The tip does not get
restated — it gets demonstrated, and the reader is the one who joins it up.

**Rules for this part, following from that:**

1. **Do not restate ChefTip #1.** The connection is the reader's to make. Naming it
   here would spend the retrospective understanding B30 exists to create.
2. **The loss of the kitchen is rendered, not lamented.** *"Part of the soul has
   gone"* is his line and it is enough. No elegy, no industry eulogy.
3. **The new work is not redemption.** Turn up, do the job, go home. It is smaller
   on purpose, and the smallness is the point.
4. **It reaches the present day.** This is the only part of the manuscript written
   from now rather than from memory. Register will differ, and that is correct.
5. **Madison is a living minor.** She is ten. What goes on the page about her is
   Bosco's call alone, and no role drafts detail about her that he has not stated.

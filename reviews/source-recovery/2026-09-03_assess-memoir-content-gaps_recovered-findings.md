## Critical extraction finding

The safe recovery boundary is intra-section, not heading-based.

The front of `HIGH RISK OF REOFFENDING` contains prison/parole-board material already superseded by promoted Chapters 01–04. However the unique post-incarceration bridge begins inside that same movement at:

> `Got released early. Good behaviour.`

Therefore a mechanical instruction such as `retire the first third` or `discard HIGH RISK OF REOFFENDING` is unsafe. It could delete unique release and post-release prose.

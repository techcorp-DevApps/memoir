# Diary of a Chef

A culinary memoir chronicling a professional kitchen journey across New Zealand and Australia.

---

## About

This memoir combines **instructional ChefTip Episodes** with **Reflective Memory Entries** to explore both the technical craft and emotional landscape of professional cooking—the dark humour, loyalty, camaraderie, and shared misery that defines kitchen culture.

The work follows a chronological arc from early life through a demanding New Zealand vineyard kitchen (0900-0300 hours during wedding season, everything made from scratch), rapid career progression to Chef de Cuisine at age 24, and the transformation of a failing 150-cover bistro.

---

## Structure

The memoir is organised into six parts:

1. **THE LONG ROAD TO NOWHERE** — Pre-kitchen origins
2. **VINEYARD ERA** — New Zealand foundations
3. **TRANSITION / AUSTRALIA** — Desert resort and bistro transformation
4. **MELBOURNE / MID-CAREER** — Italian fine dining
5. **LATE CAREER / RETROSPECTIVE** — Industry reflections
6. **EXIT** — Final chapter
